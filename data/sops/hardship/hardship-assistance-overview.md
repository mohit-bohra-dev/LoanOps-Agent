# Hardship Assistance Overview

## Available Programs
1. Forbearance (standard and disaster).
2. Repayment plan.
3. Loan modification.
4. Payment deferral.
5. Short sale (pre-foreclosure).
6. Deed-in-lieu of foreclosure.

## Rep Role
Reps can:
- Describe program features and eligibility hints.
- Collect hardship information.
- Route to Loss Mitigation for formal evaluation.
- Provide status on pending loss mitigation applications.

Reps cannot:
- Approve or deny a program.
- Guarantee eligibility.
- Advise borrower which program to choose.

## Escalation Criteria
Route to supervisor if:
- Borrower expresses confusion or frustration with options.
- Borrower mentions attorney or legal action.
- Borrower is in active bankruptcy.
- Borrower has a pending complaint.
