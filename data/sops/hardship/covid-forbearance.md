# COVID-19 Forbearance

## Overview
COVID-19 related forbearance follows the CARES Act provisions. While the national emergency has ended, borrowers experiencing COVID-related hardship may still be eligible under certain circumstances.

## CARES Act Protections
- No late fees during forbearance.
- No negative credit reporting for payments made per agreement.
- Foreclosure moratorium protections (state-specific).

## Repayment Options After COVID Forbearance
1. Repayment plan: spread missed payments over up to 12 months.
2. Payment deferral: missed payments due at loan maturity or sale.
3. Loan modification: capitalize arrears into new loan terms.
4. Prepayment: borrower may pay catch-up amount at any time.

## Key Policy
Do not offer a specific repayment method. Present all options and let borrower choose. Refer to loss mitigation for formal evaluation.
