# Disaster Forbearance

## Overview
Borrowers in FEMA-declared disaster areas may qualify for disaster forbearance regardless of loan investor.

## Eligibility
- Property located in declared disaster area.
- Borrower or co-borrower impacted by disaster.
- No requirement for delinquency at time of disaster.

## Terms
- Up to 90 days initial forbearance.
- Extension up to 12 months with periodic review.
- No late fees during disaster forbearance.
- Interest continues to accrue.

## FEMA Declaration Verification
Use FEMA disaster declaration database to confirm:
- Disaster number.
- Declared counties.
- Declaration date and effective period.

## Post-Forbearance Options
Same as standard forbearance: repayment plan, deferral, modification.
