# Repayment Plan

## Overview
A repayment plan spreads missed payments over a fixed period in addition to the regular monthly payment.

## Eligibility
- Borrower is past due but has resolved the hardship.
- Borrower has sufficient income to pay the regular payment plus a portion of arrears.
- Typically offered for 3 to 12 months.

## Plan Terms
- Arrearage divided by number of months in plan.
- Example: 3 months missed ($2,000 each) across a 6-month plan = regular payment + $1,000/month.
- Plan must be in writing and signed by borrower.
- No late fees during active plan.

## Default on Plan
If borrower misses a payment during the plan:
- Plan is cancelled.
- Full arrearage becomes due.
- Loan moves to loss mitigation evaluation.
