# Military Service Members (SCRA)

## Overview
The Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA) provides protections for active-duty military personnel.

## SCRA Interest Rate Cap
- Interest rate is capped at 6% during active duty.
- Applies to obligations incurred before active duty.
- Must be requested in writing with a copy of military orders.
- Benefit applies retroactively to the date of active duty.
- Servicer must forgive interest above 6%, not defer it.

## SCRA Foreclosure Protection
- Foreclosure is prohibited during active duty and for 12 months after.
- Applies to all loans originated before active duty.
- Borrower must provide notice and a copy of orders.
- Court may stay proceedings for the duration of active duty plus 90 days.

## SCRA Requirements
- Request SCRA benefit in writing.
- Provide copy of military orders.
- Benefit applies to all co-borrowers as well.
- No late fees during SCRA period.

## Military Spouse Protections
- Borrowers with deployed spouses also protected.
- Spouse may act on behalf of service member with power of attorney.
