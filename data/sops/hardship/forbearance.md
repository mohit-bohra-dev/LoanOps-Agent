# Forbearance Program

## Overview
Forbearance allows a borrower to temporarily pause or reduce monthly payments due to a qualifying hardship.

## Qualifying Hardships
- Job loss or reduction in income.
- Medical emergency or illness.
- Natural disaster (FEMA-declared).
- Death of co-borrower.
- Military deployment.
- Divorce or separation.

## Forbearance Terms
- Initial term: up to 6 months.
- Extension: up to 12 months total with review.
- Payment reduction: full suspension or partial (25%/50% reduction).
- Interest continues to accrue during forbearance.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Loan must be owned or serviced by us.
- Borrower must attest to hardship.
- No active loss mitigation plan.
- Property must be owner-occupied (exceptions for disaster areas).

## Documentation Required
- Borrower hardship attestation.
- Supporting documentation (job loss letter, medical records, FEMA declaration number).
- Financial worksheet.
