# New York-Specific Policies

## New York Foreclosure Laws
- Judicial foreclosure required.
- 90-day pre-foreclosure notice required before filing (RPAPL 1304).
- Settlement conference required for owner-occupied, subprime or high-cost loans.
- Foreclosure timeline: 12-18+ months from filing to sale.
- Right of redemption: until sale date (no post-sale redemption).

## New York Foreclosure Relief
- Mandatory foreclosure mediation program in certain counties.
- COVID-19 recovery: mandatory loss mitigation review before foreclosure filing.
- Borrower must receive hardship declaration form with pre-foreclosure notice.
- Filing fee reduction for military borrowers.

## New York Tenant Protection
- Foreclosed tenants have right to remain under lease (protecting tenants in foreclosure act).
- New owner must provide 90-day notice to vacate.
- Rent stabilized and rent controlled properties have additional protections.

## New York Property Tax
- School tax relief (STAR) exemption available.
- Senior citizens exemption available for age 65+.
- Property tax is a local function (county and city).
- Tax liens may be sold to private investors.
