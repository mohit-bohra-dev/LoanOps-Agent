# Florida-Specific Policies

## Florida Foreclosure Laws
- Judicial foreclosure required.
- Notice of lis pendens filed with court.
- Borrower has 20 days to respond to complaint.
- Fore sale timeline: approximately 6-12 months from filing.
- Right of redemption: up to date of foreclosure sale (not after).

## Florida Hurricane Relief
- Hurricane season: June 1 to November 30.
- FEMA disaster relief applies for named storms.
- Disaster forbearance available for hurricane-impacted areas.
- Florida-specific relief: Florida Hurricane Catastrophe Fund reinsurance.

## Florida Insurance
- Windstorm insurance required in certain coastal counties.
- Citizens Property Insurance: state-backed insurer of last resort.
- Flood insurance may be required in flood zones (NFIP or private).
- Insurance rates are regulated by Florida Office of Insurance Regulation.

## Florida Homestead Exemption
- Unlimited homestead exemption per Florida Constitution.
- Protects primary residence up to 160 acres (rural) or 1/2 acre (urban).
- Does not protect against mortgage foreclosure or tax liens.
- Applies to bankruptcy proceedings.
