# California-Specific Policies

## California Foreclosure Laws
- Non-judicial foreclosure is the primary method.
- Notice of Default (NOD) recorded after 30 days delinquency.
- 90-day reinstatement period after NOD.
- Notice of Trustee Sale recorded 20+ days before sale.
- Right of redemption: 3 months after sale (judicial only).

## California Relief Programs
- California Mortgage Relief Program: state-funded assistance for COVID hardship.
- California Foreclosure Prevention Law (SB 1079): requires investor to own property for 6 months before resale if bid less than 67% of value.
- CalHFA loans: special loss mitigation options for CalHFA-insured loans.

## Homeowner Bill of Rights (HBOR)
- Single point of contact required during loss mitigation.
- Dual tracking prohibited (foreclosure cannot proceed during active loss mitigation review).
- All denials must include specific reasons and available appeal rights.
- Records must be kept for 2 years.

## Disaster Relief (CA)
- State of Emergency declarations by Governor.
- Disaster forbearance available for declared emergencies.
- Wildfire, earthquake, and mudslide most common in CA.
