# Texas-Specific Policies

## Texas Foreclosure Laws
- Non-judicial foreclosure is the primary method.
- Notice of sale must be posted at county courthouse 21+ days before sale.
- Notice must be filed with county clerk and sent to borrower via certified mail.
- Foreclosure may be filed within days of default (no pre-filing notice period like CA).
- No statutory right of redemption for homestead after non-judicial sale.
- Judicial foreclosure: 2-year redemption period for non-homestead property.

## Texas Property Tax
- No state property tax; taxes are local (county, city, school district).
- Property tax protest process: file by May 15 or within 30 days of notice.
- Seniors 65+ may defer property taxes.
- Texas has strong property tax exemptions for homesteads ($40k+ school exemption).

## Texas Disaster Relief
- Severe weather, hurricane, and wildfire declarations.
- Texas Department of Insurance handles insurance claims.
- Disaster forbearance available per investor guidelines.

## Texas Homestead
- Unlimited homestead equity protection (Texas Constitution).
- Home equity loan restrictions: 80% LTV max, 12-year minimum term.
- No home equity loans on agricultural properties.
- Recission rights: 3 business days for home equity loans.
