# Ohio-Specific Policies

## Ohio Foreclosure Laws
- Judicial foreclosure required.
- Complaint filed in county court of common pleas.
- Borrower has 28 days to respond to complaint.
- Foreclosure timeline: approximately 6-12 months.
- Right of redemption: until sheriff's sale confirmation by court.

## Ohio Foreclosure Mediation
- Certain counties offer foreclosure mediation programs.
- Cuyahoga County: mandatory mediation for owner-occupied properties.
- Franklin County: voluntary mediation program.
- Mediation may result in loan modification or other workout.

## Ohio Property Tax
- Property taxes are assessed at county level.
- Tax bills are semi-annual (due February and July).
- Ohio homestead exemption for seniors 65+ and disabled veterans.
- Property tax delinquencies may result in county tax lien sales.

## Ohio Disaster Relief
- Severe storms, flooding, and tornadoes most common.
- Governor declares state of emergency; FEMA determines individual assistance.
- Disaster forbearance available per federal guidelines.
