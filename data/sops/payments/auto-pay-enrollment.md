# Auto-Pay Enrollment

## Overview
Borrowers may enroll in automatic ACH payments from their checking or savings account. Auto-pay deducts the monthly payment on the due date.

## Enrollment Process
1. Verify borrower identity.
2. Collect voided check or bank letter.
3. Enter bank routing and account number into servicing system.
4. Set deduction date (1st or 15th of month).
5. Confirm enrollment via system-generated notice.

## Disclosures Required
- Authorization runs until canceled.
- Variable deduction amounts if escrow changes.
- 3-business-day advance notice for amount changes.

## Cancellation
- Borrower may cancel at any time.
- Processing requires 3 business days before next scheduled deduction.
