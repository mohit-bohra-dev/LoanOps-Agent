# Payment History Inquiry

## How to Look Up Payment History
1. Navigate to the loan servicing system.
2. Enter loan ID to access the payment history screen.
3. Default view shows last 12 months of activity.

## Information Available
- Payment receipt date and amount
- Escrow disbursement records
- Fee postings
- Suspense balances and dates
- Principal curtailments

## What Not to Disclose
- Do not disclose full bank account numbers or routing numbers.
- Do not disclose other borrower's payment history on joint loans.
- Mask any personal identifying information per PII policy.
