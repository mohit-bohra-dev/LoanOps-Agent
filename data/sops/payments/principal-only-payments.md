# Principal-Only Payments

## Overview
Borrowers may make additional principal payments to reduce the loan balance faster.

## Rules
- Principal curtailments are accepted at any time.
- Curtailment must be a separate payment or explicitly designated.
- Designation: include note or select "principal only" in portal.
- Minimum curtailment: $25.

## Application
- Curtailment is applied after the regular monthly payment.
- It reduces the outstanding principal balance.
- Next payment due date is NOT advanced (curtailment does not skip a payment).
- Monthly payment amount does not change (except for ARM recast).

## Recasting
Recasting (re-amortization) requires:
- Minimum curtailment of $10,000.
- Loan must be current.
- Fee: $250 (may vary by investor).
- Available once per 12-month period.
