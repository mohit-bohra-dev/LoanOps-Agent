# Payment Disputes

## Overview
Payment disputes arise when a borrower claims a payment was made but not credited, or credited incorrectly.

## Investigation Process
1. Verify payment details: amount, date, method.
2. Check for pending/uncleared payments.
3. Verify loan number on the payment.
4. Request proof of payment from borrower (bank statement, confirmation number).

## Common Disputes
- Payment applied to wrong loan: research and reapply.
- Payment not received: request bank trace (ACH: 5 business days).
- Wrong amount applied: review payment posting breakdown.
- Duplicate payment: refund the duplicate within 10 business days.

## Escalation
If dispute cannot be resolved within 15 business days, escalate to Payment Research Team.
