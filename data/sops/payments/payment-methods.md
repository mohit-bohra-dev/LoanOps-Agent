# Accepted Payment Methods

## ACH (Automated Clearing House)
- Most common method.
- Free for borrowers.
- Processing time: 1-2 business days.
- Recurring or one-time.

## Check by Mail
- Make payable to the servicer, not the investor.
- Include loan number on memo line.
- Allow 5-7 business days for mail delivery and processing.
- Lockbox address provided in monthly statement.

## Debit Card
- Accepted via automated phone system and borrower portal.
- Processing fee: 2.75% (third-party processor).
- Daily limit: $5,000.

## Wire Transfer
- Domestic and international accepted.
- Bank wire fees apply (borrower responsibility).
- Same-day processing if received before 2 PM ET.
- Wire instructions provided upon request.

## Not Accepted
- Cash (in-person offices do not accept cash payments).
- Credit cards (debit only).
- Cryptocurrency.
- Third-party payment services (PayPal, Venmo, CashApp).
