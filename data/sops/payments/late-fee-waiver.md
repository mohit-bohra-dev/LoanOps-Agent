# Late Fee Waiver Policy

## Eligibility Criteria
One courtesy late-fee waiver per rolling 12-month period.

## Waiver Process
1. Verify borrower has not used waiver in last 12 months.
2. Confirm payment was received within 30 days of due date.
3. Process waiver in servicing system.
4. Document reason code in notes.

## Compelling Circumstances
Additional waivers may be approved by a supervisor for:
- Natural disaster (FEMA-declared counties)
- Medical emergency with documentation
- Servicer error (payment applied incorrectly)
- Military deployment (SCRA eligible)
