# Payment Processing

## Overview
Standard operating procedure for processing borrower payments. Payments may be made via ACH, check, wire transfer, or phone/debit card.

## Payment Application Order
Per RESPA guidelines, payments must be applied in the following order:
1. Suspense balance (if any)
2. Late fees
3. Interest due
4. Principal due
5. Escrow advance
6. Remaining to principal curtailment

## Late Payment Handling
- Payments received after the 15th calendar day of the due month are considered late.
- A late fee of 5% of the principal and interest payment applies.
- One late-fee waiver per rolling 12-month period is permitted at rep discretion.

## Partial Payments
- Partial payments less than the full monthly amount go into suspense.
- When accumulated suspense reaches a full payment, it is applied.
- Exception: Loss mitigation plans may have scheduled partial payments.

## Payment Confirmation
- ACH and card payments receive real-time confirmation numbers.
- Check payments require 3 business days for clearing before confirmation.
- Confirmation should include: payment amount, date applied, principal/interest/escrow breakdown, next due date.
