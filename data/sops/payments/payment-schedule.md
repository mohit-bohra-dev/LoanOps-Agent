# Payment Schedule

## Standard Amortization
Principal and interest payments are based on a fixed amortization schedule. The P&I component remains constant for fixed-rate loans throughout the payment term.

## Requesting a Payment Schedule
- Reps may provide the next 3 scheduled payments at borrower request.
- For detailed historical schedules, direct borrower to the borrower portal.
- Do not provide projected payoff amounts; refer to the Payoff Quote team.

## Payment Changes
Payment amounts change when:
- Escrow analysis results in surplus/shortage adjustment.
- Interest rate adjusts (ARM loans only).
- Loan modification or repayment plan is active.
