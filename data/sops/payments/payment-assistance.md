# Payment Assistance Programs

## Overview
Various federal and state programs provide payment assistance to eligible borrowers.

## Federal Programs
- HAF (Homeowner Assistance Fund): state-administered grants.
- COVID-19 relief: expired for new applications.
- USDA direct assistance: for USDA-serviced loans.

## State Programs
- California: California Mortgage Relief Program.
- New York: NYS Homeowner Assistance Fund.
- Florida: Florida HAF (Our Florida).
- Texas: Texas Homeowner Assistance Fund (TXHAF).
- Ohio: Ohio Homeowner Assistance Fund (OH HAF).

## Rep Role
- Provide information about available programs.
- Direct borrower to program website for application.
- Do not complete applications on behalf of borrower.
- Do not guarantee approval or funding availability.
