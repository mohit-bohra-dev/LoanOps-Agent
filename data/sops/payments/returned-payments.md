# Returned Payments (NSF)

## Overview
When a payment is returned by the bank (NSF, closed account, stop payment), the payment is reversed and fees may apply.

## Returned Payment Fee
- Fee: $25 per returned item.
- Maximum one fee per returned payment.
- Fee may be waived at rep discretion (one per rolling 12 months).

## Re-presentment Policy
- ACH payments: may be re-presented one time.
- Check payments: may be redeposited one time if bank policy allows.
- Re-presentment occurs 3 business days after return notice.

## Consequences
- Payment is reversed and due again.
- Late fee may apply if payment was after due date.
- Automatic payment enrollment may be suspended after 2 returned payments.
- 3 returned payments in 12 months may result in auto-pay revocation.
