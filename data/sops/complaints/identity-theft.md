# Identity Theft and Fraud

## Overview
If a borrower reports suspected identity theft or unauthorized account access, the case must be escalated immediately.

## Immediate Steps
1. Stop any pending disbursements or loan advances.
2. Place a fraud alert on the account.
3. Escalate to Fraud Operations team.
4. Do not discuss specific account details with caller (they may not be the real borrower).
5. Transfer to Fraud Operations for identity verification.

## Documentation Required for Investigation
- Identity theft affidavit (FTC Form 14039B).
- Police report (if filed).
- Proof of identity (government-issued ID).
- List of suspected fraudulent transactions.

## Account Actions
- Freeze account activity pending investigation.
- Issue new account number if confirmed fraud.
- Remove fraudulent transactions from account.
- Provide written confirmation of resolution.

## Reporting
- File report with FTC at IdentityTheft.gov.
- File report with local police department (if financial loss).
- Report to credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) at borrower request.
