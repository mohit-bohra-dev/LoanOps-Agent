# Complaint Handling

## Overview
All borrower complaints must be logged, tracked, and resolved within regulatory timelines.

## Complaint Categories
1. Servicing: payment posting, escrow, billing disputes.
2. Collections: harassment, timing, amount.
3. Loss mitigation: application processing, denials.
4. Foreclosure: notices, timeline, stay.
5. Fraud: identity theft, unauthorized access.
6. Regulatory: CFPB, QWR, NOE, attorney correspondence.

## Handling by Type

### CFPB Complaints
- If borrower mentions CFPB complaint: escalate to Executive Resolution immediately.
- Log complaint in system with CFPB reference number.
- Response required within 15 calendar days (extendable to 30 with notice).

### Qualified Written Request (QWR)
- Must be in writing and identify the account.
- Response required within 30 business days (RESPA).
- Request for information or explanation of charges.

### Notice of Error (NOE)
- Must identify specific error under RESPA.
- Response required within 30 business days.
- Error categories: payment application, payoff amount, escrow analysis, foreclosure.
