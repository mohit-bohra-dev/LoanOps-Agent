# Quality Assurance and Call Monitoring

## Overview
All calls are recorded and subject to quality assurance review. Minimum 3 calls per rep per month are reviewed.

## QA Scoring Criteria
1. Greeting and identification.
2. Verification of borrower identity.
3. Active listening and empathy.
4. Accuracy of information provided.
5. Correct policy/procedure application.
6. Appropriate escalation decisions.
7. Professional tone and demeanor.
8. Proper call wrap-up.

## Call Scoring
- Each criterion scored 1-5.
- Minimum acceptable score: 3.5 average.
- Below minimum: coaching session required.
- Consistently below minimum: performance improvement plan.

## Dispute Resolution
- Reps may dispute a QA score within 5 business days.
- Supervisor reviews disputed scores.
- Final decision documented in QA system.
