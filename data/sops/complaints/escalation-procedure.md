# Escalation Procedure

## When to Escalate
Reps must escalate immediately if:
- Borrower mentions CFPB, attorney general, BBB complaint.
- Borrower expresses suicidal ideation, self-harm, or threats.
- Borrower claims identity theft or unauthorized account access.
- Borrower mentions bankruptcy or active litigation.
- Borrower requests supervisor to register a formal complaint.

## Escalation Routing
| Scenario | Route To |
|----------|----------|
| CFPB / Regulatory complaint | Executive Resolution Team |
| Safety concern | Floor Supervisor + Security |
| Identity theft / Fraud | Fraud Operations |
| Bankruptcy / Legal | Legal Department |
| General complaint | Complaint Resolution Team |

## Documentation
All escalation contacts must be logged in the servicing system with:
- Time and date.
- Escalation reason.
- Person/team routed to.
- Follow-up timeframe.
