# Payoff Information

## Overview
Payoff information provides the borrower with an explanation of what would be required to fully satisfy the loan. This is informational only and not a binding payoff quote.

## What Reps Can Provide
- Current principal balance.
- Per diem interest amount.
- Estimated payoff components.
- Explanation of how payoff is calculated.
- General timeline for payoff processing.

## What Reps Cannot Provide
- Binding payoff quotes (must come from Payoff Quote team).
- Rate quotes or APR for a new loan.
- Advice on whether to refinance.
- Advice on whether to pay off the loan.

## Payoff Components
A standard payoff includes:
1. Unpaid principal balance.
2. Accrued interest through payoff date.
3. Late fees (if applicable).
4. Returned check fees (if applicable).
5. Reconveyance/recording fees (California-specific).
6. Prepayment penalty (if applicable, per note).
