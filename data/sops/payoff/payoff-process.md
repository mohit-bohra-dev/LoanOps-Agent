# Payoff Process

## Requesting a Payoff Statement
- Formal payoff statements are issued by the Payoff Quote team.
- Standard processing time: 3 business days.
- Payoff statements are valid for 10-30 days (varies by investor).

## Payoff Processing Timeline
1. Payoff quote issued.
2. Funds received (wire or certified funds).
3. Funds verified and posted.
4. Reconveyance recorded.
5. Final statement mailed to borrower.

## Escrow Balance at Payoff
- Any surplus escrow balance is refunded within 20 business days.
- If there is an escrow shortage, it is deducted from payoff proceeds.
- Outstanding disbursements are deducted and paid separately.
