# Loss Mitigation Application Process

## Overview
The loss mitigation application is the formal request for a loan modification or other workout option.

## Application Steps
1. Collect financial worksheet and hardship letter.
2. Verify borrower identity and property ownership.
3. Order BPO (Broker Price Opinion) if needed.
4. Submit to loss mitigation review team.
5. Decision within 30 days of complete application.

## Required Documents
- Completed RMA (Request for Mortgage Assistance).
- Proof of income: 2 months pay stubs, 2 years tax returns.
- Financial hardship letter signed by borrower.
- Signed authorization for third-party verification.

## Incomplete Applications
- Send deficiency letter within 5 business days.
- Borrower has 30 days to provide missing documents.
- If no response, application is closed.
- After closure, borrower must reapply.
