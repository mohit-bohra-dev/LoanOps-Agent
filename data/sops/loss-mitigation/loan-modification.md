# Loan Modification

## Overview
A loan modification permanently changes one or more terms of the mortgage to make payments affordable.

## Modification Types
1. Rate reduction: lower the interest rate (may be fixed period or life of loan).
2. Term extension: extend amortization up to 40 years.
3. Principal forbearance: portion of unpaid balance deferred at 0% interest.
4. Principal reduction: rare, investor-dependent.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Borrower must document hardship.
- Completed loan application (financial worksheet).
- Hardship affidavit signed.
- Trial Payment Plan: 3 consecutive on-time payments required.

## Evaluation Factors
- Debt-to-income ratio target <= 43%.
- Capitalization of arrears into new principal balance.
- Net present value test: modification must cost less than foreclosure.

## Trial Period
- 3-month trial payment period.
- Payments must be received on time.
- After successful trial, modification is made permanent.
- Failure during trial results in loss mitigation review.
