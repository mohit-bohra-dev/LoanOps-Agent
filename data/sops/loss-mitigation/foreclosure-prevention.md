# Foreclosure Prevention

## Overview
The goal of loss mitigation is to avoid foreclosure whenever possible.

## Foreclosure Alternatives (Ordered by Preference)
1. Loan modification: permanent payment change.
2. Payment deferral: missed payments to end of loan.
3. Repayment plan: catch up over time.
4. Short sale: sell for less than owed.
5. Deed-in-lieu: voluntary transfer of property.
6. Foreclosure: last resort.

## Timeline
- At 30 days delinquent: outreach starts.
- At 60 days delinquent: loss mitigation package offered.
- At 90 days delinquent: formal loss mitigation review.
- At 120 days delinquent: foreclosure referral (if no loss mitigation option).

## Borrower Communication
- Notify borrower of all available options.
- Provide clear instructions for application.
- Maintain single point of contact.
- No dual tracking during active review.

## State-Specific Protections
- CA: Homeowner Bill of Rights prohibits dual tracking.
- NY: mandatory settlement conference.
- FL: judicial foreclosure with longer timeline.
- OH: county mediation programs available.
