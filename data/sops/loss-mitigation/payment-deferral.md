# Payment Deferral

## Overview
Payment deferral moves the total missed payment amount to a non-interest-bearing balloon due at loan maturity, sale, or refinance.

## Eligibility
- Borrower can resume full monthly payments.
- Missed payments occurred during forbearance.
- Loan is not in active foreclosure.

## Terms
- Deferred amount: sum of missed principal, interest, taxes, insurance (PITI).
- Interest rate on deferred amount: 0%.
- Due: loan maturity, property sale, or refinance.
- No monthly payment impact.

## Documentation
- Deferral agreement signed by borrower.
- Confirmation of reinstatement of regular monthly payment.
- Disclosure that deferred amount is due in full upon triggering event.
