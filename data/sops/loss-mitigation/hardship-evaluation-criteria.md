# Hardship Evaluation Criteria

## Evaluation Factors
Loss mitigation applications are evaluated based on:

### Financial
- Income stability and sufficiency.
- Debt-to-income ratio (DTI).
- Assets and reserves.
- Employment status.

### Property
- Occupancy status (owner-occupied preferred).
- Property condition (BPO or appraisal).
- Estimated market value.
- Investor requirements (Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, FHA, VA, USDA, portfolio).

### Hardship
- Type and severity of hardship.
- Duration and expected recovery.
- Documentation quality.
- Borrower cooperation.

## NPV Test
Investors require a Net Present Value (NPV) test:
- Compare expected return of modification vs. foreclosure.
- If NPV of modification > NPV of foreclosure, modification is preferred.
- NPV result is non-binding but strongly recommended.

## Decision Outcomes
- Approved: proceed to trial payment plan.
- Denied: provide specific reason and appeal instructions.
- Counter-offer: different modification terms.
- Return for more information: incomplete application.
