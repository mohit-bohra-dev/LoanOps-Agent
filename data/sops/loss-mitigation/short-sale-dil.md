# Short Sale and Deed-in-Lieu

## Short Sale
A short sale allows the borrower to sell the property for less than the outstanding loan balance, with the lender agreeing to accept the proceeds.

### Eligibility
- Borrower is in default or default is imminent.
- Property is not in active foreclosure (varies by state).
- Borrower has documented hardship.
- Property listed with a licensed real estate agent.
- Sales price approved by investor.

## Deed-in-Lieu of Foreclosure (DIL)
Borrower voluntarily transfers property ownership to the lender.

### Eligibility
- Borrower is in default.
- No other liens on property (or subordinate liens agree to release).
- Property is not occupied by tenants (varies by state).
- Borrower has no bankruptcy filing in last 12 months.

## Cash for Keys
If borrower vacates property in broom-swept condition, a relocation assistance payment of $2,000-$5,000 may be offered.
