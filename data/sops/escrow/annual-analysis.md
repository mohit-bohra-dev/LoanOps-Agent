# Annual Escrow Analysis

## Overview
Each year, the escrow account is analyzed to ensure sufficient funds to pay projected tax and insurance disbursements. This is required by RESPA Section 10.

## Timeline
- Analysis occurs annually at least 30 days before the escrow computation year.
- Analysis statement is mailed within 30 days of recomputation.
- Borrowers receive a detailed breakdown of any surplus or shortage.

## Surplus Handling
- Surplus over $50: refunded to borrower within 30 days.
- Surplus under $50: credited to next year's escrow at borrower option.

## Shortage Handling
- Shortage under one month's escrow: optional lump sum or spread over 12 months.
- Shortage over one month's escrow: borrower may pay lump sum or spread over 12 months.
- If shortage exceeds $100 and borrower declines, mandatory 12-month spread applies.

## Cushion Limit
Per RESPA, maximum cushion is 1/6 of annual disbursements (2 months).
