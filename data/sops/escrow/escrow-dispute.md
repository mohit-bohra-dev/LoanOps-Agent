# Escrow Dispute Resolution

## Overview
Borrowers may dispute escrow analysis results, including shortage amounts or disbursement errors.

## Dispute Handling Steps
1. Review the escrow analysis worksheet with borrower.
2. Verify projected tax and insurance figures against actual bills.
3. Confirm cushion calculation is within RESPA limits (max 1/6 of annual).
4. Investigate any disbursement errors.

## Common Dispute Reasons
- Tax bill increased more than projected: provide tax authority details.
- Insurance premium changed: verify renewal notice.
- Shortage too large: restructure into longer spread period.
- Disbursement timing: confirm when payments were made.

## Resolution Timelines
- Initial response: 10 business days.
- Full resolution: 30 business days.
- If borrower not satisfied: advise written complaint to CFPB or state regulator.
