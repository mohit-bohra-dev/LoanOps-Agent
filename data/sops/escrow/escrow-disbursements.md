# Escrow Disbursements

## Disbursement Schedule
- Property taxes paid from escrow before the delinquency date.
- Hazard insurance paid at policy renewal.
- Flood insurance paid at policy renewal.
- Mortgage insurance paid monthly.

## Disbursement Inquiry
Reps can provide the following to borrowers:
- Last 2 tax disbursements (amount, payee, date).
- Last insurance premium payment (amount, insurer, effective date).
- Next scheduled disbursement (estimated amount, expected date).

## Tax Disputes
If borrower disputes tax amount:
1. Confirm tax authority and parcel number.
2. Advise borrower to contact county tax assessor directly.
3. Do not adjust escrow without county-issued corrected bill.
