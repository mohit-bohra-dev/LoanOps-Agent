# Voluntary Escrow Removal

## Eligibility
- Conventional loans only (FHA/VA require escrow).
- Loan must be current (no delinquency in last 12 months).
- LTV <= 80% based on current appraisal or BPO.
- No escrow delinquency in last 12 months.
- Borrower must request in writing.

## Process
1. Borrower submits written request.
2. Order BPO or accept current appraisal.
3. Verify LTV <= 80%.
4. Confirm no tax or insurance delinquencies.
5. Provide escrow close-out statement.
6. Disburse remaining escrow balance to borrower.
7. Add note that borrower is now responsible for taxes and insurance directly.

## Risks to Borrower
- Lump-sum tax and insurance bills.
- Late payment penalties if bills are missed.
- Potential for forced placement insurance if lapse in coverage.
- Service member protections (SCRA) still apply.
