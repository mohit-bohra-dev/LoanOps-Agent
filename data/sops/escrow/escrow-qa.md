# Escrow Q&A for Reps

## Common Borrower Questions

### Why did my escrow payment go up?
Causes include:
- County property tax reassessment.
- Hazard insurance premium increase.
- Prior-year shortage being collected.
- New escrow items added (flood insurance, PMI).

### Can I remove escrow?
Eligibility for escrow removal:
- Conventional loans: may request when LTV <= 80%.
- FHA loans: MIP required for life of loan; escrow removal not permitted.
- VA loans: escrow required per VA guidelines.

### When is my next analysis?
Annual analysis is scheduled per escrow computation year. Reps can provide the month of next analysis from system.
